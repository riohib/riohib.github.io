I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of ECE at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by [Dr. Vince Calhoun](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=WNOoGKIAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Dr. Sergey Plis](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=nm3liowAAAAJ&hl=en). My doctoral research has focused on sparse learning across different paradigms, including supervised deep learning, multimodal, federated, and reinforcement learning. I've been lucky to explore some of these ideas during internships at FAIR MetaAI, Dolby Labs, Cohere, and currently at Google DeepMind, where I am focusing on representation alignment in diffusion models. I have a broad interest in learning algorithms and intelligence. If any of that sounds interesting, please feel free to reach out to chat or learn more about my research!
